Our story

Before Forever… There Was Us ❤️

Before we talk about forever, let's go back to where “us” began.

We were just friends in college. Two people living their own lives, with absolutely no idea what life had planned for us.

Then somehow, life put us in the same company. Even there, we weren't immediately close. And then, slowly… something changed.

We talked more. We roamed around. We laughed. We shared things. And somewhere along the way, I found something I had never really felt with anyone else — comfort.

With you, I could be completely myself. I felt safe. And you felt safe with me too. That feeling became something neither of us wanted to lose.

We were both so used to giving to other people that we didn't really know what it felt like to be on the receiving side. Then we found each other… and learned how beautiful it can be to receive care, attention and love too.

Two years gave us conversations, adventures, arguments, teasing, stupid moments, beautiful moments and memories I wouldn't trade for anything.

Then came the biggest step — our families. We stood together, talked, convinced them, and slowly turned “us” into a future that two families could share.

Two years behind us. A lifetime ahead of us.

Our story didn't begin with love at first sight. It began with friendship. Friendship became comfort. Comfort became love. And love is becoming forever. ❤️
